# Privacy

Scheduled Tab Reloader is designed to do one job without observing your browsing.

## Data collection

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains
no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, remote code, or network requests of
its own.

## Website access

The extension does not request host permissions and does not inject scripts into
websites. It cannot read or modify the contents of the pages you visit.

## Local storage

Active schedules are stored in Firefox's `storage.session` and disappear when the
browser closes. The last-used form values are stored in `storage.local` for
convenience. This information stays inside Firefox.

## Permissions

- `tabs` is used to show tab titles in the picker and reload selected tabs.
- `alarms` is used to schedule reloads.
- `storage` is used for active schedules and form preferences.

No account is required.
